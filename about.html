My story

Why IT, and where I'm headed

Why Applied Computer Science?

Combining logic with passion

I chose Applied Computer Science because it brings together the things I naturally enjoy most: analytical thinking, problem-solving, and working with technology. I have always been someone who enjoys tackling complex challenges and thinking logically through problems.

I wanted a path where I could keep developing these skills and work on challenging problems every day. IT felt like the right choice because it combines logic, technology, and continuous learning.

Future dreams & ambitions

Building toward the future

My long-term goal is to work as a software engineer or backend developer - ideally in a company where quality and craft actually matter to the team. I'm drawn towards systems that need to be reliable, scalable, and well-designed rather than just functional.

Short term: I want to explore different areas of IT, continue learning how technology can solve real-world problems, and gain practical experience that prepares me for a professional development role.
