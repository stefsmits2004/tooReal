My story

Why IT, and where I'm headed

Why Applied Computer Science?

The click that changed everything

My path into IT wasn't a planned decision — it was a realisation. During my final year of secondary school I spent weekends building small scripts and browser tools just to see if I could. That compulsive need to build and fix things made the choice obvious.

Applied Computer Science at Thomas More appealed to me because it's hands-on from day one. I didn't want to spend years on theory before touching a real project. Here, you build things — and you build them fast.

Future dreams & ambitions

Where I want to end up

My long-term goal is to work as a software engineer or backend developer — ideally in a company where quality and craft actually matter to the team. I'm drawn towards systems that need to be reliable, scalable, and well-designed rather than just functional.

Short term: I want to deepen my knowledge of backend development, get comfortable with cloud infrastructure, and contribute to a meaningful open-source project before I graduate.
